Know every autopay before it hits your account

Paywatch reads the payment messages your bank already sends you, finds your autopays, UPI mandates and subscriptions, and reminds you before each debit. It also shows you how to stop the ones you no longer want.

Private by design. Your SMS are read only on your phone. Message text is never saved or uploaded.

What it does

  • Finds autopays, e-mandates, NACH debits and subscriptions from bank SMS
  • Reminds you before each debit, with optional voice alerts
  • Warns you about price increases and charges after you cancelled
  • Shows step-by-step ways to stop an autopay in your UPI or bank app
