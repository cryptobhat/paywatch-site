Delete your Paywatch account

Paywatch by Nagaraj Bhat (Astravanta)

In the app

Open Paywatch → Settings → Delete account, then confirm. Your account is deleted straight away and all Paywatch data is removed from your phone.

Without the app

Email hello@astravanta.com with the subject “Delete my Paywatch account” and the phone number you signed in with. We will confirm the request and delete the account within 7 days.

What is deleted

  • Your phone number and name
  • Your sign-in record
  • The link between your account and usage events (the events themselves become anonymous)

Your SMS-derived data (autopays and transaction summaries) is stored only on your phone and is never on our servers. It is removed when you delete your account in the app or uninstall Paywatch.

Paywatch uses the same sign-in as our other apps. Deleting your account deletes it for all of them.
